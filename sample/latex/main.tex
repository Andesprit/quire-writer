\section{Starter}\label{sec:starter}
A starter is flour and water that wild yeast and bacteria have colonised. Its activity
roughly doubles for every ten degrees of warmth, as Equation~\ref{eq:rate} shows.

\begin{equation}\label{eq:rate}
  r(T) = r_0 \cdot 2^{(T - T_0)/10}
\end{equation}
